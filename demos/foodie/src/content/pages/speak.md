You do not need to be a professional, and your dish does not need to be fancy. Some of our best nights came from someone showing us the one thing they make really well.

## What we look for

Something you are excited to share: a family recipe, a technique, a cheap-and-cheerful weeknight win. First-time demos are especially welcome.

## Quick demos welcome

Not ready for a full session? A ten-minute "here is how I do this" is a great way to dip a toe in.

## We will help you prep

Tell us roughly what you want to cover and an organizer will help you plan the shopping, the timing, and the kit. You will not be figuring it out alone.

## Put your hand up

Send us your idea through our [sign-up form](https://example.com/cfp), or [say hi in the chat](https://example.com/chat) and we can talk it through.
