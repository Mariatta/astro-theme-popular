You do not need to be an expert, and your pick does not need to be popular. Some of our best nights came from someone championing a show nobody else had seen yet.

## What we look for

A short intro to a drama, a themed quiz, a "why this OST lives in my head" ramble, anything you are excited to share. First-time hosts especially welcome.

## Lightning slots welcome

Not ready to host a whole night? A five-minute recommendation between episodes is a great way to start.

## We will help you prep

Tell us roughly what you want to cover and an organizer will help you shape it, from the clip list to the timing. You will not be figuring it out alone.

## Put your hand up

Send us your idea through our [sign-up form](https://example.com/cfp), or [say hi in the chat](https://example.com/chat) and we can talk it through.
